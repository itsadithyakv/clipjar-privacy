# Clipjar privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Clipjar is a clipboard history app for Windows. This policy explains, in plain
words, what happens to your information. The short version: **everything stays
on your PC, and nobody (including us) ever sees it.**

## What Clipjar stores

Clipjar saves the things you copy, so you can paste them again later: text,
links, rich text, images (if you turn image saving on), lists of copied files,
and the name of the app you copied from. It also stores your Clipjar settings.

All of it is kept in Clipjar's own private folder on your PC
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<Clipjar>\LocalState`). You can open that folder from
**Settings > About > Data folder**.

## What Clipjar never does

- It never sends your clipboard, your history or your settings anywhere. Clipjar
  has no servers, no account, no cloud sync and no network code of its own.
- It contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no ads and
  no third-party tracking libraries.
- It never saves copies that the source app marks as private. Password
  managers such as 1Password, Bitwarden and KeePass do this, and they are also
  on Clipjar's ignore list by default.

## Choices you have

- **Private mode** (tray menu or a shortcut) pauses saving for 15 minutes, an
  hour, or until you turn it back on.
- **Ignore list**: copies from the apps you choose are never saved.
- **Skip passwords and card numbers**: text that looks like a password or a
  payment card number isn't saved (on by default).
- **Delete** any item, or **Clear history** in Settings, at any time.
- **Auto-delete**: keep history for a set number of days or items.

## Your Microsoft Store license

Clipjar is sold through the Microsoft Store. To know whether you are in the free
trial or have bought the app, Clipjar asks Windows for its license. Windows
answers from the license it keeps on your PC, and the Microsoft Store app keeps
that license up to date as part of Windows. That exchange is between Windows and
Microsoft and is covered by the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
It contains nothing from your clipboard.

## Uninstalling

Uninstalling Clipjar deletes its folder, which removes your whole history and
all settings from the PC.

## Children

Clipjar collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
